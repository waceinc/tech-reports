Of the two readings considered, the more inclusive one is used, because a bound should err high; under the literal reading (a unit whose own request needed more than 40 minutes is left out, so T08 is not counted) the values are given side by side. The units counted under this rule are the six units that waited (T10, T12, T13, T14, T15, T16) and T07 and T08 (on the processing time of their repetition-2 requests, outside the slow stretch of that evening). Of these nine, T07, T10 and T16 were judged in the sensitivity analysis and did not pass (1) and (2), and T12's answer carries a correct-block comment, which leaves 5 units whose result is unknown (T06, T08, T13, T14, T15).
